## Part 2: Who You're Competing With

We analyzed three primary competitors: **jagerpro.com**, **gamechangertraps.com**, and **pigbrig.com**. Here's what we found.

### The competitive landscape is small and defined

There are only a handful of brands competing seriously for this keyword space. The SERP for "wild hog trap cameras" shows:

- **JAGER PRO** — named alongside HogEye in the AI Overview. Cellular trap camera system. Strong content presence on their own product terms.
- **Game Changer Traps** — trap-first brand. The Vision Cam is secondary to their trap hardware. Good organic presence.
- **Pig Brig** — spending on ads ("Catch the Whole Sounder"). Active content strategy on bait, trap techniques, and sounder capture.

Generic trail camera brands (Reolink, Vosker, VOSPCAM) appear in the shopping carousel but **do not have content competing in the trap-specific space**. That's a structural advantage for HogEye.

### Competitor keyword analysis: 498 gaps found

We pulled the full keyword universe for each competitor and cross-referenced it against HogEye's existing coverage. Across all three competitors combined, we identified **498 keywords** that they rank for and HogEye currently does not appear for.

That sounds like a lot. It's actually manageable — most of those gaps cluster into a small number of topic areas. The important finding is this: **the gaps are not on your core product terms.** They're on content topics that sit one step away from the product — trap types, baiting strategies, hog behavior, damage costs, regulations.

In other words, competitors aren't outranking you on what you sell. They're just publishing more content around the problems your buyers have before they go looking for a camera.

## Part 3: What Your Audience Is Actually Asking

We extracted the **People Also Ask** questions that appear across 35 target search queries. These are the questions Google surfaces because real people are asking them and nobody has answered them definitively.

**61 unique questions** were found. The most important for HogEye:

**Questions that HogEye is uniquely positioned to answer:**
- *"How often should I check my hog trap?"* — This question is directly solved by remote monitoring. Nobody has written a great answer for it that routes to a trap camera solution.
- *"What are common hog trap mistakes?"* — Appeared in PAA results for **5 different search queries**, the highest frequency in our dataset. High featured snippet potential.
- *"What is the best location to place hog traps?"* — Operational guidance nobody has owned yet.
- *"How long does it take hogs to find bait?"* — Directly relevant to the conditioning window and why you need a camera to know when the sounder has committed.

**Questions that reveal the buyer's earlier journey:**
- *"How much damage do feral hogs cause?"* / *"How much does feral hog damage cost?"* — Buyers who don't yet know trapping is the solution. Answering this well puts HogEye at the start of the purchase journey, not just the end.
- *"Do I need a license to trap hogs in Texas?"* — Regulatory content nobody in the commercial camera space has written. High authority signal for Texas-based buyers (your largest market).

**The gap these questions reveal:** your competitors have published content about their products. Nobody has published genuinely useful, problem-solving content that serves the hog trapper as a professional. That's the gap HogEye can own.

## Part 4: The Strategic Opportunity

### You're not starting from behind

Most clients at this stage are trying to break into a competitive space. HogEye is already in it — named by Google, ranking organically, with an established keyword universe. The work ahead is expansion, not establishment.

### The niche is an advantage, not a limitation

The trap camera market is small by design. But that means:
- Fewer brands fighting for the same content space
- Lower keyword difficulty on high-intent terms
- A more defined audience that shares content within specific communities (Facebook groups, ranch management forums, wildlife damage control networks)
- Less noise to cut through when you publish something genuinely useful

### The content gap is winnable in 90 days

The 498 keyword gaps we found collapse into roughly **15–20 distinct topic areas**. We don't need to write 498 pieces. We need to write 15–20 well-structured, authoritative pieces that cover those topics comprehensively. At 10 pieces per month, that's achievable in two cycles.
