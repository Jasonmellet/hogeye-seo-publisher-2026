# HogEye Editorial Workflow

This workflow extends the existing monthly package process instead of replacing it.

## Sequence

1. Ingest source material into `sources/`.
2. Generate derived notes when transcript-based source material exists.
3. Build or update the monthly queue.
4. Create the brief.
5. Build the research pack.
6. Draft the article.
7. Run the humanizer pass.
8. Run validator and QA.
9. Prepare Google Docs handoff.

## Integration rule

Derived notes are a quality upgrade layer:

- preferred for transcript-backed content
- optional for purely website/data-led updates
- never a replacement for research pack evidence

### Camera Installation and Maintenance Tips
- Always mount the HogEye camera straight up and down on the T-post to prevent water ingress. The camera head can be adjusted to angle the video downwards after removing the dome with a counterclockwise twist. If the dome is difficult to remove or reattach, take a step back and reassess the process to avoid damaging the tabs.
