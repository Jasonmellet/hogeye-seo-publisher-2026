### Maximizing Cellular Connectivity
- The Omni directional antenna included with all HogEye camera systems is effective for over 95% of users regarding cellular connectivity. To enhance performance, mount the antenna as high as possible on a T-post, as height improves connectivity chances. Users within eight miles of a cell tower typically achieve good connectivity with the standard antenna. For those facing connectivity issues due to topography, consider using a 30-foot antenna cable to elevate the antenna.
