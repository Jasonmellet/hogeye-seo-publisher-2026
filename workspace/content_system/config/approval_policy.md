# HogEye Approval Policy

Phase 1 stops at approval-ready content packages.

## Human-gated policy

- No artifact becomes canonical just because an LLM wrote it.
- No content should move toward WordPress until brief, research pack, draft, humanizer pass, and QA are complete.
- Human review is required before anything is considered ready for client review or phase-2 WordPress staging.

## Approval-ready minimum

An article package is approval-ready only when:

- the brief passes North Star checks
- the research pack contains real evidence
- the draft stays inside approved HogEye positioning
- the final draft does not add facts
- the QA file is marked PASS with no unresolved blockers

## Out of scope for phase 1

- automatic live publishing
- silent prompt-driven doctrine rewrites
- automatic conversion of draft output into canonical approved content without review
