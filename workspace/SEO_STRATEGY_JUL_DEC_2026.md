## 1. Strategic thesis (why this arc)

The audit shows HogEye is **brand-strong, category-narrow**: ~49% of GSC clicks are brand; only **137** non-brand clicks total; DataForSEO Labs returns **25** ranked keywords post-niche-gate vs **259** for Spartan (`findings/dataforseo-research-summary.md`). Generic 90k/mo "cellular trail camera" SERPs are owned by YouTube/Amazon/Spartan and are the **wrong fight**.

The winnable ground is **purpose-built hog trap monitoring**, where HogEye already wins (41/320 SERP top-20; ChatGPT cites HE on 7/28 product-intent tasks) plus **three open frontiers** with proven demand and zero/low HE presence:

1. **Screwworm × remote monitoring** — 6,600/mo "what is screwworm" (LOW competition); HE cited **0/5** ChatGPT screwworm tasks; **0** GSC screwworm queries → pure greenfield + stakeholder mandate.
2. **Comparison authority** — ChatGPT already cites HE on the comp-stack; GSC strikers on `vosker vs reolink` (3 clicks, pos 2.4) and `hogeye vs boarbuster` (185 imp, pos 10).
3. **Geo monitoring** (TX 40%; MS/LA/OK/AL 60%) — buy-intent state SERPs surface trap *dealers*, not camera brands; GSC barely present → net-new.

**The 6-month move:** Jul–Aug built the foundation and proved each frontier (July live, August in WP drafts). Sep–Dec continues at **5 posts/mo**, scaling clusters that show traction and pulling from the **parked overflow pool** (`workspace/content_pipeline/monthly/2026-09/parked/`) when each month opens.

## 5. Screwworm program — dedup & longevity (critical risk)

Contract demands 2–3 screwworm posts/mo, but `stakeholder/wildlife-dominion/screwworm-topic-matrix.md` defines only **4 core themes** shared across three brands. At 13 HogEye screwworm posts over 6 months, themes will exhaust fast. To stay unique vs Boar Blanket / Big Pig **and** avoid self-cannibalization, expand along **HogEye-only axes** (all stay on the monitoring/observation lens — never vet/clinical, never trap-type which belongs to BB/Big Pig):

- **Geo axis:** same monitoring angle, different state context (TX, MS, LA, OK, AL) — unique slug/H1 each.
- **Seasonal axis:** fly-pressure windows, calving/farrowing season awareness, year-end outlook.
- **Operational axis:** unattended trap-line check-in SOPs, carcass/bait fly-pressure monitoring, cattle-vs-wildlife visibility gap.
- **Format axis:** landowner FAQ (AI-Overview-targeted), checklist, field guide, program/SOP.

**Guardrails (every screwworm post):** *"If it's on shit, that isn't it"* (NWS breeds on live animals); hogs move ~6 mi/day and attract flies; cite USDA APHIS / extension for clinical facts; `trust_sensitive: true` — educational only. Coordinate every title/slug at the WD umbrella matrix before briefs finalize.

**Standby:** Schell's screwworm **microsite + custom tracker** may trigger a rapid pivot (`stakeholder/.../screwworm-topic-matrix.md`). If triggered, brand-blog screwworm cadence may shift to the microsite — keep brief templates ready and treat the monthly count as reallocatable. Also pending: **Schell podcast material** to enrich briefs.
