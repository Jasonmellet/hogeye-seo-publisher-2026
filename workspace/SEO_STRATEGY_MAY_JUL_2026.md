# HogEye SEO Content Strategy: May–July 2026

**Generated:** 2026-04-17  
**Research basis:** DataForSEO keyword universe (270 site keywords), competitor gap analysis vs. jagerpro.com / gamechangertraps.com / pigbrig.com (498 gaps found), PAA extraction across 35 seed queries (61 unique questions)

## Strategic Shift

March and April content built the product-education layer: camera setup, trap compatibility, trigger reliability, multi-user monitoring. That work is done. The next 3 months pivot to **topical authority across the full hog management lifecycle** — capturing searchers earlier in the funnel (problem-aware, solution-comparing) before they even know they need a trap camera.

**The through-line for every piece:**  
Feral hog damage is expensive → systematic trapping is the solution → remote monitoring is what makes trapping reliable at scale.

Every piece must still pass the North Star test. None of this is generic wildlife content. It is trap-outcome content that routes through HogEye as the monitoring and closure layer.
