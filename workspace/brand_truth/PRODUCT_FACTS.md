## How a HogEye trap closes

- The landowner watches the trap live in the HogEye app, counts the hogs inside and outside, and when the whole sounder is inside sends the close command from the phone. The gate closes.
- The landowner makes that call. The camera does not decide or close on its own.
- Closing on part of a sounder teaches the rest to avoid the trap.
