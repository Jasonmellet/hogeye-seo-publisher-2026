### Messaging guardrails (confirmed)

- **Phrase to use:** *“If it’s on shit, that isn’t it”* — New World screwworm (NWS) **only breeds on live animals** (possibly recently deceased); feces is not a breeding substrate.
- **Do not** position any brand as veterinary/medical authority for treatment protocols.
- **Do** emphasize: ranchers watch cattle; **wildlife and feral hogs are harder to monitor**; hogs **move ~6 miles/day** and **attract flies**; wildlife at risk.
