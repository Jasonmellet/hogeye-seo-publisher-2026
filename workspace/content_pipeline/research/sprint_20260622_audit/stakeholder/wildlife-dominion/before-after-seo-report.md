## DataForSEO validation (Jun 2026 pull)

Third-party Labs data **confirms the GSC story** — especially for Boar Blanket’s content breadth — and adds SERP/LLM context where Google exports alone are thin.

### Boar Blanket — strong content-library signal

- **~639 organic keywords** and **~$966/mo** estimated traffic value (Labs, Jun 2026).
- **29+ editorial URLs** carry measurable rankings beyond the homepage; top Labs ETV pages include Georgia hog-traps hub, NC deer-season/hogs post, Big Pig Trap comparison, whole-sounder guide, and state FAQs — matching GSC top click winners.
- **SERP top-10 presence** on GSC-validated non-brand queries including *hog blanket*, *boar net*, *voorhies hog trap*, *hog traps for sale louisiana*, *passive hog trap net*.
- **LLM citations:** HogEye appears in Google AI mention index for *wild hog monitoring camera system* and *net camera trap remote hog trapping*; Boar Blanket indexed for *best hog trap for large sounders* (category research queries).

### HogEye — brand-led with growing content surface

- **~34 ranking keywords**; **~$559/mo** Labs ETV — concentrated on homepage/brand terms (expected for a product-led site).
- Content URLs earning Labs visibility include `/net-camera-trap-remote-hog-trapping/` and comparison posts; GSC still shows stronger click volume on `/hog-traps/` and guides than Labs ETV alone suggests (GSC = actual clicks; Labs = modeled).
- **SERP top-10 wins** on *hog camera*, *hog trap camera*, and brand-adjacent queries; comparison intent (*vosker vs reolink*, *remote hog trap trigger*) still competitive — content opportunity remains.
- **LLM:** `hogeyecameras.com` cited for monitoring-camera and net-camera-trap queries.

## When we started & when traffic showed up

**Q3 2025** — All Great Things began the current SEO/content program (comparison guides, how-tos, state/regional pages, trap-education hubs).

### HogEye Cameras

- **GSC history** begins Jan 26, 2025. Pre-program (Jan–Mar 2025): **1,187** organic clicks. **Apr 2025 onward:** **7,435** clicks through Jun 7, 2026.
- **Monthly GSC clicks** climbed from **476** (Apr 2025) toward **694** (Oct 2025), then held **~375–600/mo** in 2026.
- **GA4 organic** (property `245859533`): **~850 sessions/mo** in Q1 2025 vs **~750/mo** averaged Apr 2025–May 2026 — stable with higher engagement on newer content paths. (Note: GA4 shows a **2024 organic spike** unrelated to this content program; excluded from before/after framing.)
- **First content URLs earning search clicks** include `/hog-traps/` (**147** GSC clicks), `/net-camera-trap-remote-hog-trapping/` (**52**), and comparison/off-grid guides.

### Boar Blanket

- **GSC data starts Apr 1, 2025** — matches program launch. **First full month:** **36** organic clicks (Apr 2025).
- **Growth:** **157** (May) → **380–392** (Sep–Oct 2025) → **1,868** (Nov 2025 peak) → **~245–640/mo** in 2026.
- **Impressions** exceeded **60,000/mo** at peak (Nov 2025), showing broad query visibility across state and education content.
- **Top search winners** are net-new editorial URLs — not just the homepage: e.g. feral hog trapping costs (**438** clicks), Boar Blanket vs Pig Brig (**381**), whole-sounder guide (**149**).

## HogEye — what blog & new pages delivered

- **Discovery on intent-rich URLs** — Trap and camera-education pages rank and earn clicks in Google (hog-traps hub, net-camera trap guide, Reolink vs Vosker vs HogEye comparison).
- **Stable qualified organic** — GA4 shows **~600–1,000** organic sessions per month post-launch with **63%+ engagement rate** on organic (2026 manual export window), higher than paid search.
- **Commercial support** — GSC clicks also hit `/buy-now/`, `/camera-login/`, `/steel-camera/`, and shop paths alongside educational entry points.
- **Business takeaway** — Content fills the research funnel for trap-camera buyers; search visibility is concentrated on hog-specific URLs rather than generic homepage traffic alone.

---

## Boar Blanket — what blog & new pages delivered

- **From zero to measurable program** — Organic clicks grew from **36/mo** (Apr 2025) to **1,868/mo** (Nov 2025) on the strength of published guides, comparisons, and state pages.
- **Content-led clicks** — **6,000+** GSC clicks in the program period; top URLs are long-form education and comparison posts, not only `boarblanket.com/` (**3,011** clicks — homepage still #1, but **~50%+** of measurable clicks spread across **150+** deeper URLs).
- **State & FAQ coverage** — Louisiana/Texas/Oklahoma hunting and trapping FAQs, shop-by-state hub, and “vs competitor” posts capture regional and consideration queries.
- **Business takeaway** — SEO built a **searchable content library** that introduces Boar Blanket during research (cost, methods, comparisons) before shoppers reach product and shop pages.

## Combined takeaway

Wildlife Dominion’s Q2 2025 SEO program produced **two different but complementary wins**: Boar Blanket shows **strong click growth from a near-zero baseline** driven by net-new educational and comparison URLs; HogEye **maintained solid organic volume** while **new pages earned targeted GSC clicks** on hog-trap and camera-comparison intent. GSC confirms both brands rank content beyond the homepage — the program is working as a **research-and-consideration layer** for landowners and operators.
