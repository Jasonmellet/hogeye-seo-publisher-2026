## Shared facts (all brands)

- NWS **only breeds on live animals** (possibly recently deceased) — **not on feces**. Phrase: **“If it’s on shit, that isn’t it.”**
- Hogs move **~6 miles/day**; **attract flies**; **wildlife at risk**; ranchers monitor **cattle** but not **wildlife** equally.
- **Texas** ~40% ad spend; **MS, LA, OK, AL** ~60% combined — weave geographically where natural.
- Cite **USDA APHIS / extension** for clinical facts; no brand is veterinary authority.
