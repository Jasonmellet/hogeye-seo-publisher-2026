## Planning implication for Sept content

- **MS velvet (Sept. 11)** and **LA early archery (Sept. 19)** are already inside the September publishing window — deer-prep monitoring copy should assume some landowners are *in* season, not only pre-season.
- **TX / OK / most of AL** still have a September *prep* window before archery (TX Oct. 3, OK Oct. 1, AL Oct. 1 or 15).
- Gun openers cluster around **Nov. 7 (TX)** and **Nov. 21 (MS/OK/AL private gun)** — October posts can carry “last month before gun” if Sept takes the plot/feeder/remote-check theme.

## Notes / caveats

- Youth, muzzleloader, primitive, and CWD-zone seasons are omitted from the opener columns; see the source pages.
- TX mule deer archery also opens **Oct. 3, 2026** (Panhandle through Nov. 20; Trans-Pecos through Nov. 26) on the same TPWD page.
- Always re-check the linked agency page before a brief cites a date in published copy.
