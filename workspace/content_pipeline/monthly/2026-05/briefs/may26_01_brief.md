## Search Intent

Informational. Landowners and farmers trying to understand whether the hog problem on their property is "bad enough" to justify investment in a trapping program. They want numbers, not opinions. This is a problem-awareness piece — the reader has hogs but may not yet be in active solution mode.

---

## Angle

Lead with hard numbers. USDA estimates $1.5B+ in annual feral hog damage nationally. Break it down by: crop type (corn, soybeans, wheat, pasture), property size, and rooting intensity. Use a simple table or tiered framework.

The HogEye connection is the conclusion, not the intro: systematic trapping with remote monitoring reduces the cost of a trap program (fewer wasted trips) while increasing sounder capture rates (higher damage reduction per dollar spent). Don't force this — earn it by being genuinely useful first.
