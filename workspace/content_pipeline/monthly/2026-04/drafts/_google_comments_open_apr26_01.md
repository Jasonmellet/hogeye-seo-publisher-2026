- **Schell Gower** (2026-04-20T19:42:56.422Z): @jason@allgreatthings.io I needed to edit this to fit how we can use the Net Trap and HogEye camera together. 

The Boar Blanket or any net trap doesn't require any type of "trapping trigger" to trap hogs. It's more of a passive solution than an active monitoring and drop solution like a steel trap or gate. 

The HE makes it easier to get live updates with unlimited data, so you can look at your trip at any time without having to pay increased data fees. Once you are conditioning, you can use the HE to set the trap from conditioning mode(slightly elevated on a T-Post so hogs can slide under the net and feel the net) to active trap mode where the center of the ned is on the ground and hogs can gently push under the net. Being able to remotely trigger the net to active catch allows you to monitor if hogs are present and switch between conditioning to trapping without educating hogs. 

You still need a good plan for monitoring and tracking the presence of the entire sounder, so you can count how many you are wanting to catch at the site.
