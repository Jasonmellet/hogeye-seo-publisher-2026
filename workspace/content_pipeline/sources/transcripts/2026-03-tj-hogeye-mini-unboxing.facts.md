## Extracted facts (authoritative)

1. HogEye Mini unboxing includes full field setup components in shipped package.
2. System includes a 75 watt foldable solar panel.
3. Charge controller regulates solar-to-battery charging and battery-to-camera power.
4. Kit includes camera, charge controller, battery, and wiring/connectivity components.
5. HogEye Mini app functionality is stated as identical to previous generation in transcript.
6. Mini hardware footprint is smaller than prior versions (stated as less than half size in transcript).
7. IR is integrated into camera face around lens (no external IR lights in mini setup).
8. Mini uses a paddle-style antenna on unit body.
9. Legacy-style high-mount antenna approach can still be used in poor-service environments with adapters.
10. Mounting bracket supports T-post and posts up to 2 inches diameter.
11. Wiring harness is detachable and screws to camera bottom.
12. Moisture protection cap and tightened harness connection are part of setup process.
13. Included battery is a 50 amp hour lithium deep-cycle battery (as stated in transcript).
14. Battery and camera are stated with a three-year warranty in transcript.
15. Charge controller has yellow/blue/black cable mapping:
    - yellow: power out to camera
    - blue: battery input
    - black: solar input
16. Gate/latch integration uses a green two-pin gate cable path and latch adapter interface.
17. Tight battery terminal connections are emphasized to avoid resistance/corrosion issues.
18. Solar panel should be aimed predominantly south in northern hemisphere for better charging maintenance.
19. Standard trap-side pre-leave workflow includes full connection walkaround and test drop.
20. Final test drop is treated as required readiness confirmation step.
