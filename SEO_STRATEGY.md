## Positioning (North Star)

HogEye is a **wild hog trap camera system** — remote monitoring and trigger readiness for trap lines and land operations. Not generic cellular trail cameras, not property/security cameras. Every piece routes through HogEye as the **monitoring and closure layer** for hog trapping.

**Audience:** landowners and land managers (not "ranchers"). No security or surveillance framing.

**Screwworm line:** *"If it's on shit, that isn't it."* New World screwworm breeds on live animals. HogEye's role is remote observation and early awareness, never veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

**Cross-brand uniqueness:** HogEye = monitoring / alerts / remote trigger. Boar Blanket = landscape trapping. Big Pig Traps = removal at scale. Unique titles and slugs on screwworm and deer-season topics.
